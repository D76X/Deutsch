# Pumpking and ricotta cake

primo composto:
-500 g ricotta -2 uova
-100 g zucchero -60 g farina
-vaniglia
mettiamo la ricotta, lo zucchero, un po di vanialia
le uova
mix it all
ad the flour
mix well again

- 2nd
ingredienti secondo composto:
-300 g zucca cotta
-sale
-40 g zucchero -2 uova
-60 g farina -scorza di limone
crash the cooked cold pumpkin to a pulp
add the eggs, the sugar, a bit of salt, the lemon rind
mix it all with the blender
add the flour
mix to an homogenous cream
prepare the baking non-stick stamp i.e 20cm diam.  by brushing some oil on the inside
then sprinkle some flower on the oily inside of the baking vessel

Add the compounds in layers one after the other with partial overlaps to create a marble effect
bake at 170 for 50 to 60 mins
