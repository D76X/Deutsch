# Our Lady of Kibeho aka  Our Lady of Sorrows

Our Lady of Kibeho (also known as Our Lady of Sorrows of Kibeho) is a Catholic title of the Virgin Mary based on apparitions reported between 1981 and 1989 in Kibeho, southwestern Rwanda. On June 29, 2001, the Catholic Church officially approved the apparitions, making Kibeho the first Vatican-recognized Marian apparition site in Africa. [1] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Kibeho), [2] (https://www.kibeho-cana.org/a-brief-history-of-the-apparitions-of-our-lady-of-kibeho/), [3] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XcI_-srMNlE&t=5)

Primary Visionaries and Timeline

The approved apparitions were experienced by three students at Kibeho High School: [1] (https://www.kibeho-cana.org/a-brief-history-of-the-apparitions-of-our-lady-of-kibeho/), [2] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Kibeho)
• Alphonsine Mumureke: First reported seeing Mary on November 28, 1981 (now observed as the feast day), with apparitions continuing until November 28, 1989. Mary introduced herself in Kinyarwanda as Nyina wa Jambo ("Mother of the Word"). [1] (https://theologyofhome.com/blogs/in-place-in-person/our-lady-of-kibeho), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EZOKgOFaLMY), [3] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Kibeho)
• Nathalie Mukamazimpaka: Began experiencing apparitions on January 12, 1982, continuing into 1983. [1] (https://www.kibeho-cana.org/a-brief-history-of-the-apparitions-of-our-lady-of-kibeho/), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EZOKgOFaLMY)
• Marie Claire Mukangango: Began experiencing apparitions on March 2, 1982. [1] (https://www.kibeho-cana.org/a-brief-history-of-the-apparitions-of-our-lady-of-kibeho/), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EZOKgOFaLMY)

Core Messages and Warnings

• Repentance and Conversion: Urged people to turn away from sin, practice genuine prayer without hypocrisy, and embrace love, forgiveness, and reconciliation. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AOFkIVn4DFM&vl=en&t=113), [2] (https://marian.org/articles/listen-our-lady-kibeho), [3] (https://www.amazon.co.uk/Our-Lady-Kibeho-Speaks-Africa/dp/140192378X), [4] (https://www.kibeho-cana.org/a-brief-history-of-the-apparitions-of-our-lady-of-kibeho/)
• Prayer and Devotion: Emphasized praying the Rosary and revived devotion to the Rosary of the Seven Sorrows. [1] (https://www.instagram.com/reel/Daq2gbKCjZu/), [2] (https://www.kibeho-cana.org/a-brief-history-of-the-apparitions-of-our-lady-of-kibeho/), [3] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AOFkIVn4DFM&vl=en&t=113)
• Warnings of Violence: Visionaries reported distressing visions of bloodshed and conflict—most notably in August 1982—warning of severe tragedy if hearts were not softened. Many Catholics and observers connect these warnings to the 1994 Rwandan genocide. [1] (https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2019/jan/14/our-lady-of-kibeho-royal-and-derngate-northampton-rwandan-genocide-katori-hall), [2] (https://marian.org/articles/listen-our-lady-kibeho), [3] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AOFkIVn4DFM&vl=en&t=113)

Shrine and Legacy

The Sanctuary of Our Lady of Kibeho in southern Rwanda draws pilgrims for prayer, healing, and reconciliation, with pastoral care entrusted to the Pallottine Fathers. The events are also the subject of books such as Immaculée Ilibagiza's Our Lady of Kibeho and Katori Hall's stage play Our Lady of Kibeho. For more background, see Our Lady of Kibeho or the CANA history of the apparitions.
